# Wave 6 event audit specification (2026-11-14)

English distribution summary of the specification recorded before event counting.
Use verified QC data and full-precision frozen coefficients. Baseline memory >4,
follow-up memory <=4; refusal and death are not events. Exclude actual development
participants; report 2021/2022/2023 separately. The 42-54 month sample is the
four-year target, with 45-51 months and exclusion of the entire source cohort as
sensitivities. Reintroducing date conflicts is only a sensitivity analysis.
Do not interpret all 5,901 participants as a common four-year risk set.
Report Wilson and 2,000 community-bootstrap event proportion intervals, and
frozen predicted risk by follow-up status. The community-bootstrap seed is
20261114 plus the sample fingerprint. No refitting, AUC, Z-score outcome or
ordinary IPCW treatment of deaths in this stage. Different horizons are not
same-horizon probability calibration. Original development: 4,441/658 events.
